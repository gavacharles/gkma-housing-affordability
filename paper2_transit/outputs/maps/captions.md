## P2_affordability_premium
**The Expressway premium on a listed house, in years of the catchment's median household income.** Premium = value × (1 − e^−β) for houses within 15 minutes of an Expressway access point; catchment median household income UGX 707,465 a month (18 parishes, 167,599 households, modelled from UNHS 2019/20 and Census 2024). Sources: RED listings 2017–2026; OpenStreetMap road network; UBOS Census 2024; UNHS 2019/20.
