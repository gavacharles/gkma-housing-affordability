# Figure captions (draft)

## P4_01_yields
**Rental yields and house-price growth against the cost of money.** (a) Sub-counties with at least 20 rental and 20 sale listings; rents and prices are medians per bedroom from different dwellings, so yields are indicative. Benchmarks are 12-month means to August 2026. (b) Quarterly, 2015-07 to 2026-04. Sources: Listings (RED, Jiji, Uganda Property Centre); Bank of Uganda via the MoFPED Macro Data Portal; UBOS Residential Property Price Index.

## P4_02_plot_and_build
**Years of saving to buy a plot and build in stages, against saving a mortgage deposit.** Plot at the sub-county's median asking price per decimal (sub-counties with at least 20 land listings); a 2-bedroom 50 m² house: shell UGX 58 million, finishes UGX 39 million (CAHF benchmarks indexed to 2026). Self-build starter: UGX 35 million (assumption). Saving 20% of median household income, without interest or cost growth. Sources: Land listings (RED, Jiji, Uganda Property Centre); CAHF (2020) indexed with the UBOS CIPI; modelled parish incomes (UNHS 2019/20, Census 2024).

## P4_03_plot_years
**Price of a standard 50 × 100 ft plot in years of median household income, by sub-county.** Median asking price per decimal × 11.48 decimals, divided by the sub-county's annual median income. Sources: Listings: RED, Jiji, Uganda Property Centre (asking prices, deduplicated); boundaries: UBOS (parishes 2016); basemap: OpenStreetMap; lake: OSM.
